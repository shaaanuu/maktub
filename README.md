# Maktub ✨📝

**"It is written. Now, let’s get it done."**

Maktub is your personal task manager with a touch of destiny. It’s not just about organizing your day—it’s about writing your own story, one task at a time.

## What Does Maktub Do?

- Helps you write your to-dos like they’re written in the stars.
- Keeps you focused with a clean, intuitive interface.
- Makes checking off tasks feel oddly satisfying.

## Why Maktub?

Because every great journey starts with a plan, and Maktub makes sure you stick to it. Plus, it sounds cooler than “To-Do App.”

## Features

- **Task Management**: Add, edit, delete, and conquer your to-dos.
- **Minimal & Elegant**: Destiny doesn’t need clutter.
- **Satisfaction Guaranteed**: Few things in life feel as good as ticking off a task.

## How to Use

1. Write your tasks.
2. Do your tasks.
3. Tick them off and feel like a productivity wizard.

## Warning

Maktub won’t do the tasks for you, but it’ll make you feel destined to finish them.
